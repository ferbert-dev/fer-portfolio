Questions before the first call

The six things companies ask us first. Yours is not here? Ask it directly.

Ask us a question
Do we need an AI strategy before we talk to you?

No. You need one process that costs your team too many hours. The process analysis turns that into a ranked list of what to automate first, with cost and expected savings.

How fast will we see something working?

The process analysis takes two to three weeks. The first agent is live [N] weeks after that, depending on the process and the tools it connects to.

Will agents replace people on our team?

Agents take over the repetitive steps: reading, checking, entering data, answering routine questions. Unclear and sensitive cases go to your team, and people approve what matters.

What happens to our data?

We build on EU-hosted infrastructure and EU-region AI models, and show you where each part runs. We sign a data processing agreement before any personal data touches an agent. Read our privacy notice

Are we locked in after the project?

No. Code, prompts, data and accounts belong to you. If you take the agents in-house, we hand everything over cleanly.

What if an agent gets something wrong or stops?

We build agents with retries, monitoring and human review for unclear cases, and every action is logged. With “Run and improve” we watch the agents and fix them when a tool or a model changes.

Which process should run itself first?

It starts with a free 30-minute call. We’ll look at your processes together and tell you honestly what is worth automating.
