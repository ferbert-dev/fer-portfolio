# Home page (/, /de)

Scope: the home page of the freelance/studio site, plus the shared chrome (nav, footer) that later pages inherit. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: SMB owners/ops managers, startup leads, engineering managers deciding whether to hire Igor for AI automation or software work.
Action: start a conversation about what to automate (contact page; hero carries a working entry point).
Belief after first screen: AI can save us money; his AI runs in production; he is a safe senior engineer.
Proof on hand: Telegram News Agent in production (numbers to come from Igor), demo recordings to come. No testimonials or client logos exist; none may be invented.
Constraints: English + German. Not dark/hacker, not corporate, not playful, not static.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a technical drawing sheet. A client's process is drawn as a dimensioned assembly, measured in hours, and a revision redraws it automated. It refuses the AI-agency default: dark ground, neon glow, chat-bubble mockups, cards of buzzwords.

OWN-WORLD: White drafting film, black ink in ISO 128 line weights (thick outlines, thin dimension and hatch lines), revision red for every "after" state and the primary action, non-photo blue for construction lines and secondary info. DIN-style engineering lettering. Title block (Schriftfeld) grid, revision table, section markers A–A, balloon callouts, centre lines. No shadows, no gradients, no rounded cards.

STORY: The visitor sees their kind of process drawn and measured, sees it revised with AI to a fraction of the hours, learns it is built with production engineering (section view), sees real systems Igor runs, and writes to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width sheet with drawing frame and grid references. Left two thirds: an isometric/orthographic assembly of an invoice-intake process, dimension lines reading hours per week; a red revision cloud and "Rev B" redraw it with an agent, dimension now 4 h. Headline set as the drawing title, large DIN lettering, upper left. Bottom-right title block holds name, studio, availability and the primary action "Map my process" plus a one-field input naming the task that eats the week. Lines draw once on load.

FORM: Engineering drawing sheet; candidate 5 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key ddda4cda (re-roll 1). Signature interaction: section cut A–A through the agent on scroll, exposing retrieval, guardrails, retries, monitoring.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
