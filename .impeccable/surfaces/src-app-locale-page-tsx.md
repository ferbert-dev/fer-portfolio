# Home page (/, /de)

Scope: the home page of the Ferbert agency site, plus the shared chrome (nav, footer) that contact, blog and later pages inherit. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: owners and operations managers of small and mid-sized companies, startup leads, engineering managers deciding whether to hire Ferbert to automate work with AI.
Action: book a free call / describe a process (contact page).
Belief after first screen: AI can save us money; Ferbert's agents run in production; the engineers behind it are senior and safe.
Proof on hand: Telegram News Agent in production (numbers pending), demo recordings pending. No testimonials or client logos; none may be invented. Demos are labelled as demos with fictional companies.
Constraints: English + German. Brand "Ferbert", voice "we". Owner reference: relay.app-level polish. Not dark/hacker, not corporate, not playful, not static.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a working automation you can watch: a calm, light product surface where agents visibly run, retrieve and talk. It refuses the AI-agency default of dark neon, glowing orbs and buzzword cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white canvas (#FAFAF8) with dot-grid workspace panels, ink #18181F, one iris accent (#5B4FF5) plus a step palette (blue, violet, amber, red, green) used only for workflow nodes and steps. Onest throughout, tight negative tracking on display. Rounded 16–28px surfaces, hairline borders, soft long shadows only on live panels.

STORY: Visitors read the promise, watch an invoice automation run, try a RAG demo and a voice-call demo, see what Ferbert automates and how an engagement runs, learn the team is engineering-led, then book a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred headline "Your busywork, handled by AI agents." with subline and two buttons (book call primary, try demos), then the live workflow panel directly below, five nodes lighting in sequence with a travelling dot and a run log.

FORM: Live workflow product surface (owner-pinned reference relay.app); direction B of three shown; seed key ddda4cda. Signature interaction: the looping workflow run; demos are user-driven (question picker, play call).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
