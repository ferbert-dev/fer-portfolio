## Offer

1. **Process analysis.** Two to three weeks. Maps how work moves through a company, measures each process in hours, ranks what AI can take over by savings and risk. The client keeps the report whether or not anything is built.
2. **Agents and automation.** Customer support agents, document and email handling, company knowledge assistants, integrations and custom software.
3. **Run and improve.** Monitoring, cost tracking, fixes when a tool or a model changes, monthly improvements.

Engagement order: free 30-minute call, process analysis, build, run and improve.

## Positioning

Backend engineers with production experience (Java, Kotlin, Kafka, cloud infrastructure) who build AI agents the same way: retries, monitoring, human review for unclear cases, every action logged.

## Data protection commitments stated on the site

The site makes these four promises. The owner should confirm each one is true in practice before launch.

1. Client data stays in the EU: EU-hosted infrastructure and EU-region AI models, and the client is shown where each part runs.
2. A data processing agreement is signed before any personal data touches an agent.
3. Unclear or sensitive cases go to the client's team; every agent action is logged and can be reviewed.
4. Code, prompts, data and accounts belong to the client. No lock-in, clean handover.

## What the site must not claim

- No testimonials, client names, client logos, case-study figures or savings numbers. None exist that may be published.
- No real projects of any kind. See decision 1 in [decisions.md](decisions.md).
- No invented prices, legal details, social handles or response times. Unknown values stay as `[SQUARE BRACKET]` placeholders until the owner supplies them.
