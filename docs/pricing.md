# Pricing

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-10.

## The model

The owner charges by the hour and cannot quote build work before knowing what will be built.

1. **Process analysis: fixed price.** Agreed before the work starts. It is the low-risk way for a client to begin.
2. **Everything after it: written estimate.** The amount depends on what is automated. After the analysis the client gets an estimate in hours at the hourly rate.
3. **Approval first.** Building starts only after the client approves the estimate. The client pays for the hours worked.

## Values (decision 18, 2026-10-26)

- Process analysis: €500 fixed, one week (a couple of meetings plus a full day of analysis).
- Hourly rate: from €65.
- From the end of the analysis to the first live agent: 2–6 weeks.

## A point raised in the critique

Hourly billing with no upper limit can worry a cautious buyer. Options the owner could consider: state that the estimate is binding unless the scope changes, or name a typical range. Nothing of this is on the site; it would be a new commitment and needs the owner's decision.
