# Reference: tbdstudio.framer.ai

The owner named https://tbdstudio.framer.ai/ as a reference on 2026-10-10. This file records what was studied, what was taken and what was left out.

## What the reference is

"tbd® Workflow Studio", a paid Framer template for an automation studio. The site was captured as 23 desktop screenshots plus short load, hover and scroll sequences. Measured values:

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Page background | rgb(5, 5, 5) |
| Text face | Geist, weight 400 |
| Headline | 90 px, letter-spacing −0.04em, line-height 0.89 |
| Lead text | 16 px, white at 60 % |
| Small labels | Geist Mono, 10–11 px, weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em |
| Accent | Light green block behind one headline word |

Its motion: a centred loader with a progress bar and percent, a text effect in which characters scramble before settling (on headlines in view and on buttons at hover), a green band with scrolling labels, a button that fills from white to green at hover, and a scroll progress line.

## Used in two different ways

### In direction B: structure only
B kept its own light look. Six ideas were adapted as structure, with Ferbert's own content:

1. A pre-call FAQ.
2. Three ways to start.
3. A case layout (challenge, metric, stack, link). It was applied to the news agent panel and removed with it.
4. Numbered service rows in place of cards.
5. A cadence line in large type.
6. A marker highlight on one headline word.

### In direction D: look and motion
The owner then asked for a design that looks similar and has similar animations. Direction D takes the visual language: dark canvas, Geist and Geist Mono, one light-green accent, framed hero with a side rail, mono labels, numbered rows, line-pattern tiles, a framed closing panel, and the motion listed above.

## Left out on purpose

- The reference's photographs, logo, client logos and wording. D uses Ferbert's copy and a dot-grid and grain background drawn for it.
- Client logo strip, testimonials, star rating, "80+ systems" and similar figures. Ferbert has no publishable evidence of that kind, and the owner decided to show none.
- The inline contact form in the footer. Ferbert keeps a separate contact page.
- Coloured glow shadows. They were tried in D and removed.

## Honest differences

D is close to the reference in feel, and that was the request. It is not a copy: layout details, icons, background and all content are Ferbert's own. If the site is released in direction D, the owner should be comfortable that it resembles a template other studios can buy.

## Access note

The domain must be on the allowed list of a cloud environment before a cloud session can open it. Rendering D also needs `fonts.googleapis.com`.
